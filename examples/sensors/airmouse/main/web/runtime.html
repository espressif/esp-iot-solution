What To Do

The page will automatically advance calibration. No extra button press is required.

  • For gyroscope calibration, place the device on a stable surface before the countdown reaches zero.
  • For magnetometer calibration, slowly rotate the device through different orientations until the page reports completion.
  • Keep this page open. AirMouse will continue to BLE startup automatically after calibration finishes.
